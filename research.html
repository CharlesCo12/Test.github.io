My research spans valued differential fields with analytic structure, pseudofinite groups, and o-minimal extensions of real fields.

My favourite theorem for now is:

The direct sum of two pseudofinite groups is pseudofinite.

\[ \text{An abelian group is pseudofinite if $G$ is elementarily equivalent to an ultraproduct of finite abelian groups, } \prod\limits_{\mathcal{U}} M_n. \]
